Questions

Common questions

Something else on your mind? Ask us directly.

Do I need to have a domain already?

No. If you don't have one, we'll help you pick it and register it in your name. If you already have one, we'll connect your new site to it.

Who owns the website?

You do. The domain is registered in your name, and you get a copy of all the website files. If you ever leave, everything goes with you.

Where is your team based?

We're a remote studio working with US businesses. Our hours overlap with US mornings and afternoons, and we reply to messages within one business day.

What do you need from me?

About 15 minutes on a call, your logo if you have one, and any photos of your work or team. We write all the text for you.

Are there monthly fees?

Not for Launch or Launch + Maps. Standard hosting is included. Monthly support through the Grow plan is optional and you can cancel any time.
